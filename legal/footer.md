# SOGDIA — Trading and Execution Footer

Site-wide disclosure. Rendered at the foot of every page.

Import the canonical string from `legal/disclosures.ts` rather than copying
this text into components — one source, one place to update.

---

Trading and execution. SOGDIA operates a proprietary trading and
risk-management framework. Evaluation accounts are governed by the rules in
effect at the time of purchase. Traders who meet the stated objectives will be
offered a funded arrangement, subject to verification and the applicable
funding terms. SOGDIA maintains capital and execution partner relationships.
The execution model, spreads and fees applicable to each account are set out in
the Execution Disclosure (/legal/execution) and the applicable account terms.
Market data may be delayed, interrupted or inaccurate.

---

## Notes for whoever maintains this

- The link to `/legal/execution` must resolve. The footer is deliberately
  short and defers the execution model to that page; if the link breaks, the
  footer becomes misleading by omission.
- "will be offered" is a commitment, not a disclaimer. It is conditioned on
  verification and the funding agreement, and `legal/terms.md` §4 is worded to
  match. Keep the three in sync.
