# Execution Disclosure

Published at `/legal/execution`. Linked from the site-wide footer.

---

## How orders are executed

Orders submitted through SOGDIA accounts are executed against SOGDIA's
execution engine using reference prices obtained from third-party venues. A
spread of 0.05% is applied against the trader on entry and exit. Trader orders
are not routed to an external execution venue.

## Partner arrangements

SOGDIA maintains relationships with third-party capital and execution
partners, and may hedge or mirror aggregate exposure with those partners. Such
arrangements are separate from the execution of trader orders and do not alter
the price at which a trader's order fills.

## Funded trading

Traders who meet the objectives and risk parameters assigned to their
evaluation account will be offered a funded trading arrangement, subject to
identity verification and execution of the applicable funding agreement. The
contracting entity, capital provider, trading limits, loss allocation and
compensation terms are specified in that agreement.

## Reference prices

Displayed prices are obtained from third-party market data providers and are
not a guarantee of execution price. Market data may be delayed, interrupted or
inaccurate.

## Trading costs

Traders are responsible for the trading costs disclosed for their account.
The 0.05% spread described above is applied by SOGDIA and is not a third-party
market cost.

## Payouts

Profit payable to a trader is calculated from the trader's recorded fills and
is subject to the payout split, cycle and eligibility conditions in the
applicable account terms. The current split is 80% to the trader, over a
14-day cycle.

## Risk controls and records

Trading activity is subject to the position limits, drawdown requirements and
exposure restrictions disclosed for the relevant account. Orders, execution
reports and account activity are recorded and reconciled for risk management,
compliance, dispute resolution and account administration.
