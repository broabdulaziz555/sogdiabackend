# SOGDIA — Trading Execution, Evaluation and Capital Allocation

Published at `/legal/terms`.

---

## 1. Account Rules

The trading rules, evaluation objectives, risk limits, fee schedule and other
applicable account conditions are established and recorded at the time an
account is purchased. Each account remains subject to its applicable rule set,
except where changes are permitted under the governing terms or required by
law.

## 2. Market Data and Order Execution

SOGDIA provides a proprietary trading evaluation and capital-allocation
program. Subject to the applicable account agreement, orders submitted through
eligible accounts are routed to designated execution partners for execution in
the relevant financial markets.

Where live execution applies, the resulting transactions are subject to the
execution venue's or counterparty's available liquidity, market conditions,
trading hours, order acceptance criteria and applicable execution policies.
Orders may be partially filled, rejected, cancelled or executed at a price
different from the displayed reference price.

SOGDIA's trading interface may display market data obtained from third-party
providers. Displayed reference prices are not a guarantee of the price at which
an order will execute. Actual execution details are determined by the
applicable execution records.

## 3. Trading Costs

Traders are responsible for the trading costs disclosed for their account,
which may include spreads, commissions, financing charges, swaps and other
applicable fees.

Any additional SOGDIA execution markup or spread adjustment will be disclosed
in the applicable account terms. Such adjustments will not be described as
third-party market costs unless they are genuinely charged by the relevant
provider.

## 4. Evaluation and Qualification

Evaluation accounts are assessed against the objectives and risk parameters
assigned to the relevant account. Traders who meet those objectives will be
offered a capital allocation upon satisfying the applicable qualification,
verification and contractual requirements.

SOGDIA may review trading activity for compliance with published rules, risk
limits and applicable legal requirements.

## 5. Funded Trading and Capital Allocation

Eligible traders will be offered a funded trading arrangement through SOGDIA or
a designated capital or execution partner. The identity of the contracting
entity, capital provider, execution provider, applicable trading limits, loss
allocation and compensation arrangements will be specified in the relevant
agreement.

A funded account is subject to its written allocation terms. The account's
stated notional size does not, by itself, represent cash deposited with or
owned by the trader.

## 6. Risk Controls and Records

Trading activity is subject to applicable position limits, drawdown
requirements, exposure restrictions and other disclosed risk controls. Orders,
execution reports and relevant account activity may be recorded and reconciled
for risk management, compliance, dispute resolution and account
administration.

## 7. Disclosure of Execution Model

SOGDIA will identify whether the applicable account uses live-market
execution, simulated execution or a combination of these arrangements. No
account will be represented as executing real-market trades unless the relevant
execution arrangements support that representation.

The execution model currently applicable to all accounts is set out in the
Execution Disclosure (`/legal/execution`).
